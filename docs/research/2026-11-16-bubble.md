## 1. What Bubble does, step by step

- **Questions as choices.** "Bubble AI may ask a few questions to clarify what you're building." "Each one
  offers a set of multiple-choice options, so you can answer with a tap." "If none of them fit, you can type
  your own answer instead." [F] https://manual.bubble.io/help-guides/ai/bubble-ai-agent/generate-apps-with-ai
  The owner got three, each stating the platform's limit inside the question ("AR 치수 자동측정은 플러그인/수동
  설정이 필요해요. 대안은 가로·세로·높이를 직접 입력하는 방식입니다"), and answered all three. [O]
- **A plan to review before anything is built.** Two views: features ("each feature as its own card") and
  scenarios ("As a home cook, I can save my own recipes"), plus style; features and scenarios can be added.
  [F] same page. The owner's plan: 7 features, 3 scenarios, a visual design, and "About 7 mins" beside the
  Generate button. [O]
- **Why the planning conversation exists.** It is new (August to September 2026). "Initial app generation used
  to be a single prompt that dropped you straight into the editor." "The idea is to front-load more of that
  back-and-forth so the first version is closer to what you need." [F]
  https://bubble.io/blog/september-2026-founder-ama/
- **Generation.** "Once you confirm the blueprint, the Agent builds a working version of your app in one pass":
  "pages and components for web and mobile, visual workflows for your app's logic, a database with privacy
  rules, and sample data for testing." [F] https://bubble.io/blog/what-is-bubble-ai/ (2026-08-19). On screen the
  stages were Views, Design, Data, Logic, each with three sub-steps named in the product's own words; Views was
  done at 0:58. [O]
- **Time.** Bubble says "in minutes". A reviewer measured "around five to seven minutes for a complete MVP".
  [F] https://nocode.mba/articles/bubble-ai-app-builder (a course seller with affiliate links).
- **Cost.** "Generating an app doesn't use AI credits." Editing with the Agent does: 500 credits a month free,
  1,000 on Starter, $0.01 a credit beyond. [F] https://bubble.io/pricing , https://bubble.io/pricing/ai

## 2. Why it is fast

Stated by Bubble:

- **The model writes no code.** "it builds with Bubble's own elements, workflows, and data types, so there's no
  generated code to read." [F] https://bubble.io/support/en/articles/17351301-bubble-mcp . "it replaces
  traditional code rather than generating it." [F] https://bubble.io/faq
- **What it writes instead is a tree of typed parts.** The MCP's operations show the shape: `create_page`,
  `create_mobile_view`, `create_element`, `set_element_properties`, `create_event`, `create_action`,
  `create_data_type`, `create_field`, expressions from a fixed vocabulary (`get_expression_vocabulary`,
  `validate_expression`), applied all or nothing (`apply_changes`: "If any change fails, none of them are
  applied."). [F] same MCP page.
- **One engine runs every app.** The exported JSON "is a record of your app's structure. It can't be run outside
  Bubble". [F] https://bubble.io/support/en/articles/18337497-exporting-your-bubble-app . For phones: "We've
  migrated Bubble's mobile app engine to React Native's new architecture" and "No changes to your app logic,
  workflows, or design are required." [F]
  https://forum.bubble.io/t/bubble-mobile-apps-now-run-on-react-natives-new-architecture/391588
- **Models are picked per task** ("building UI, writing logic, and generating expressions don't all call for
  the same model"), Claude the largest share, some of Bubble's own fine-tuned ones. [F] September AMA.

What follows from that [I]:

1. Nothing is installed, compiled, bundled or deployed. The preview is the same engine in the browser.
2. Sign-in, the database, a tab bar, navigation, privacy rules are parts of the engine that the model switches
   on. It does not build them.
3. Nothing is run to see whether it works. No page describes a test of the generated app; see section 3.

No source says how many model calls a generation is or whether its stages run at the same time.

## 3. What the speed leaves undone

- **Bubble's own guide:** "Many core flows will work right away." "If a button doesn't do anything when you
  click it, the workflow trigger probably needs attention in the editor." "For trickier logic … open the
  workflow in the visual editor and set the conditions yourself." Privacy rules: "Treat them as a starting
  point, and review them yourself before you go live". [F] https://bubble.io/blog/how-to-build-an-app-with-ai/
  (2026-09-25), https://bubble.io/blog/what-is-bubble-ai/
- **No test of the result.** "The AI testing lab shown in June hasn't shipped." The MCP's `create_test_cases`
  "Adds descriptions of functional tests to the app. This doesn't run them." [F]
  https://bubble.io/blog/october-2026-founder-ama/ , MCP page. What exists is the issue checker (a check of
  the definition, required before deploying) and, since Agent 2 in October, screenshots: the Agent "runs and
  learns from the issue checker in the background as it builds", claimed "2X better at ensuring the result is
  fully functional", no method given. [F] https://bubble.io/blog/agent-2/
- **Users, on the forum:** "The screens were there. The wiring was not." "clicking submit did nothing."
  (2026-02-19) [F] https://forum.bubble.io/t/case-study-wiring-bubble-s-ai-generated-app-into-a-working-system/391653 .
  "I'm consistently getting about 10-20 errors in the issue checker after generating an app with the AI."
  (2026-05-05) [F] https://forum.bubble.io/t/bubble-ai-app-generator/395752 . "the interface generated
  initially is riddled with bugs and inconsistencies that require significant manual corrections"
  (2026-03-16) [F] https://forum.bubble.io/t/ai-agent-should-be-removed/393263
- **A reviewer:** detail pages "existed but were empty. View details buttons had no workflows attached."
  "Once Bubble generates your app, you're back to manual development." [F] nocode.mba. Another summed it up as
  the generator creating structure faster than behaviour. [S]
- **What only the outside world knows is left to the person.** In the owner's own session: "편의점·우체국 실시간
  점포 데이터는 외부 API 연동이 필요해서 생성 후 에디터에서 수동 설정이 필요합니다", and the limits and prices of
  each service are offered as something to add ("요금 기준 데이터 — 각 서비스별 세 변 합·무게·가액 한도 등 구체적인
  수치를 finite_data_values에 넣으면 더 정확한 판독 화면이 나와요"). [O] For this product those numbers are the
  product.
- **Look.** Bubble: the AI "may build your app in its own 'fingerprint' style". [F] MCP page. The owner:
  "디자인은 구리긴한데".
- **Leaving.** "Bubble doesn't export your app as ready-to-run code." "Bubble doesn't export user passwords".
  [F] export article. Publishing needs a paid plan: web $32 a month, phone $42, both $59 (billed yearly);
  workload is metered on top. [F] pricing pages.

## 5. Position: what sz takes, and what it does not

sz does not chase seven minutes. That figure is the sum of three things: no code, no check, and commodity parts
already in the engine. The check is what sz is for, and plain code is why its products can leave. What Bubble
gives in seven minutes is the kind of result the owner turned down from the studio earlier ("목업이 앱으로
들어온것같음"), unless its verdicts turn out to be right, which nobody has looked at.

Three things Bubble does better, in the order I would take them:

1. **Questions as choices, and never skipped.** Done on 2026-11-16 (`plugin/skills/work/SKILL.md`,
   `plugin/workflows/build.js`): three at most, each a choice with what it gives and what it costs, the known
   limit said inside the question; the workflow does not start until it is told whether the owner was asked.
2. **The plan and the time before the first milestone.** Done the same day: one screen (what a person will be
   able to do, milestones, what is left out, minutes to expect) and the workflow logs the estimate. The owner
   had written, with a second milestone closing, that the run had already taken an hour and 21 minutes.
3. **Commodity parts built once.** Not done; a proposal. Every sz product starts from no files, and the builder
   thinks out again a server, settings, a log, the 404 page, dark mode, link previews, a privacy page, and
   accounts when there are any. In the owner's run the whole fifth milestone (28 of 181 minutes) was such
   parts, and part of the first. A house starter per target (web; web with accounts on the house backend; a
   phone app with tabs and sign-in), its claims proven once and carried as standing claims, would take that
   out of every run and let a product start with accounts and saved history that really work. It must carry
   structure and plumbing only, never the look: the owner has already objected to products sharing a palette.
   What it buys, by this one measurement and my estimate of the first milestone's share, is about a fifth of a
   run, not a factor of ten.

Not taken: a definition run by an engine of our own (that is building Bubble), and showing every screen before
any of them works.

Open, and worth one run: a first milestone held to three claims, to see how early a true first look can come.
Not measured.
