# What only real people can answer: the cheapest ways to ask (Korea)

Read on the platforms' and the law's own pages on 2026-11-16; the full notes with sources are in `docs/research/2026-11-16-worth/real-tests-korea.md` of the harness's repository. Prices, rules and fees move: read the page again before quoting one to the owner. Nothing here is legal or tax advice; say what the page says and that it is the owner's to settle.

## Which test answers which question

| The question | The cheapest honest test | Money | The owner's hours | Enough people | Go | No |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Do they have the trouble, and what do they do about it | Five conversations about the last time it happened | 0 to 150,000 won (a small fee each) | 5 to 8 | 5 (8 if it is 2 of 5) | 3 of 5 describe it unasked **and** already spend money or an hour a week on a way round it | 1 of 5 or none |
| Will people who see the price act | A page that states the price and takes a contact, posted where they are | 0 | 1 to 2 | 300 different visitors of the kind meant | 3.8% or more leave a contact after seeing the price | under 2% |
| The same, of strangers | That page with paid clicks (about 160,000 won for 300 clicks on Naver search at about 530 won) | about 160,000 won | 1 to 2 | 300 clicks, each channel read apart | as above | as above |
| Does the outcome matter enough to come back | Do the service by hand for five people | 0 | 10 to 20 | 5 | 3 of 5 ask for a second round, or pay | 1 of 5 or none |
| **Will they pay** | An order or a deposit that takes real money (needs the owner's business registration: see `sz:backend`, Money) | fees only | 3 to 5, plus registering | 300 to 500 visitors | 10 strangers pay, or 1% of 500 strangers | 2 or fewer after 300 |
| Do they stay | The live product's own counts | hosting | ongoing | 100 active people in a group | back on day 7: 13% or more; of those who try it free, 3 to 5% pay | day 7 under 5%; under 1% pay |

A survey of a panel (about 50,000 won for 100 answers) sizes and screens; it is never a go. A listing on a platform (Apps in Toss, a browser's extension store, a KakaoTalk channel) is a way to be found, not a test.

## How the numbers are read

- **Said before, read after.** `sz-eyes worth ask ... --sample <n> --go-at <percent> --no-at <percent>` is written before anyone is asked, with a day the test ends. `sz-eyes worth heard` then answers go, no, or cannot tell. "Promising" is not an answer.
- **Fewer than a hundred: a count, never a rate** ("3 of 74"). Telling 2% from 5% takes about 300 different visitors of the kind meant (wrong about one time in thirteen; 500 makes it one in thirty). Three of five people is somewhere between a quarter and nine in ten: five people say what and why, never how many.
- **What a person gave up counts, not what they looked at:** a page view, less than a contact, less than a contact left after seeing the price, less than a press on the price, less than money, less than a second use. Likes, upvotes and "좋아요, 쓸게요" count nothing.
- **Each channel apart.** Friends and communities that know the owner say yes more than strangers do; paid clicks from strangers are the floor. Leave out the owner's own visits.
- **A list goes cold.** Of people who join a waiting list, about one in five pays if reached within a month and fewer than one in ten after three. Write to them within thirty days.

## An honest page before the product exists

- The price is on the page, and "아직 만드는 중" is said before any button. A button that looks like buying says at the press that nothing can be bought yet. No card field without a real charge.
- Taking a contact needs four things said (개인정보 보호법 15조 ②): why it is taken, what is taken, how long it is kept, that the person may refuse and what refusing costs. Consent to news and offers is a separate box, not ticked. Delete on the day that was said.
- Taking money before the thing exists is selling: it needs the owner's registration, and the page says the day of delivery as an agreement and that the money comes back within three working days if it cannot be delivered (전자상거래법 15조).

Wordings that say it plainly:

- "아직 만드는 중입니다. 출시하면 월 9,900원에 팔 예정이에요. 이 값에 쓰실 생각이 있으면 이메일을 남겨 주세요. 지금은 결제가 없고, 출시 소식 한 번만 보내 드립니다."
- After a press on a price button: "솔직히 말씀드리면 아직 결제를 받을 단계가 아닙니다. 몇 분이 이 버튼을 누르는지 보고 만들지 정하려고 해요. 출시되면 알림을 받으시겠어요? (선택)"
- "이 페이지는 만들지 말지 정하려는 실험입니다. 12월 31일까지 신청이 100명을 넘으면 만들고, 넘지 않으면 만들지 않고 받은 연락처를 바로 지웁니다."

## Where a maker may speak

- GeekNews "Show GN": only something people can try. A page that only tests demand is not allowed there.
- 디스콰이엇, Threads, X: a maker's own thing is the norm. The trouble first, real screens, a request for what people think; a polished picture reads as an advertisement and is passed over.
- 클리앙: promotion is banned outside the boards set aside for it.
- Each 네이버 카페 and open chat has its own rules: read them before the owner posts.

## What is the owner's, asked once and together

Posts under their own name; an advertising account and a card; proof of who they are where a platform asks; registering a business before money is taken; paying the people they talk to; doing the work by hand; the word go or no. Everything else (the page, the consent text, a link for each channel, the counting, the questions, the drafts of the posts in each place's manner) is prepared before they are asked, and they are asked as one list with the cost and the minutes of each.
